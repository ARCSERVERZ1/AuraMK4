FOOD QUALITY & BALANCE
==================================================

Look for: fried foods, junk food, fast food, processed food, sugary drinks, whole foods.
Identify missing food groups (Protein, Quality carbs, Veggies, Healthy fats).
Do not call foods forbidden. Use practical recommendations ("Reduce frequency", "Replace with").


==================================================
MEAL SCORE & OVERALL SCORE
==================================================

Timing       = 20%
Nutrition    = 25%
Portion      = 20%
Food Quality = 20%
Balance      = 15%

meal_score = (timing_score * 0.20) + (nutrition_score * 0.25) + (portion_score * 0.20) + (food_quality_score * 0.20) + (balance_score * 0.15)
If a category is missing, exclude it from the calculation rather than giving a zero.

80–100 = Good
60–79  = Fair
0–59   = Poor

CRITICAL RULE: You MUST evaluate the PERSON PROFILE's "long term-status" or "medical_status" (e.g., Pregnancy, Diabetes, Hypertension).

- Every recommendation MUST be safe and appropriate for their specific medical condition.
- Explicitly target nutritional needs for their condition (e.g., if pregnant, emphasize folic acid, iron-rich foods, and avoiding raw foods/late heavy meals).
- Keep recommendations short. Prefer 1–2 important actions formatted as a text string with bullet points (•).
